# Intoch

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Restaurant owners, managers, and operators in Indonesia. Staff record reservations,
walk-ins, and actual visits; managers and owners use guest history and reports.

## Product Purpose

Connect customer data across restaurant visits so teams can recognize guests,
maintain relationships, and support retention.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Existing vanilla HTML/JavaScript app with per-client Supabase projects. Reservations,
walk-ins, membership, deposits, invoices, vouchers, reports, and explicit WhatsApp
follow-up. Opening WhatsApp is not automatic delivery. Preserve routes, verified
authorization, existing interactions, and generated configuration boundaries.

## Brand Commitments

The name is **Intoch**, lowercase c. Preserve the actual logo. The landing page's
primary Indonesian CTA remains **Coba Intoch Sekarang**. The user approved white
and uniform dark navy content sections, near-black footer, soft rounded panels
and pill controls, selective headline emphasis, and compact tabbed mobile content.
AccelByte's restrained dark-blue contrast is the supplied visual reference.

## Evidence on Hand

Existing landing-page workflow simulations and actual logo assets. Demo values are illustrative; the user requested removal of repeated visible
sample-data labels. Generated mockup portraits and replacement logos are not
product assets. No verified outcome statistics or new commercial commitments were
provided for the redesign.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Indonesian default and English toggle. Readable mobile demonstrations, keyboard
tabs, visible focus, pause/replay, and reduced-motion alternatives are required.
