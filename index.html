Staff

Who can log in, and what they are allowed to see.

What each role can do

  • Staff — the front desk. Dashboard, reservations, walk-ins, membership and broadcast. Can see areas and the dish list but cannot change them.
  • Manager — everything staff can do, plus prizes, thresholds, branding, and deleting a reservation or voiding a walk-in.
  • Admin — the owner. Everything a manager can do, plus the owner dashboard and this screen.

Accounts are never deleted, only deactivated. A deactivated person cannot log in, and the visits, walk-ins and vouchers they recorded keep their name on them. Deleting the account would erase that history.
