# Landing page

Target: `landing.html`. Mode: Persuade. Approved 2026-10-08 after desktop/mobile
mockup review; this contract incorporates the final single-navy and rounding corrections.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Demonstrate how reservations, walk-ins, and return visits become one guest
history. Short explanations and usable demos take priority over repeated marketing cards.

OWN-WORLD: White canvas, one uniform dark navy section color, near-black footer,
logo-blue actions, pale-blue emphasis on navy. Rounded panels and pill controls;
no lighter blue shell around dark sections. Preserve actual Intoch assets.

STORY: Recognize the customer-data problem, inspect practical restaurant scenarios,
understand onboarding, and contact the team through Coba Intoch Sekarang.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left-aligned two-tone sans headline and brief copy beside a large
sample guest history; one primary pill CTA and quiet secondary link. Phone retains
the same hierarchy with a compact readable example.

FORM: User-pinned direction, approved through iterative visual mockups; no new
concept selection is required. Three feature tabs and four scenario choices replace
long phone stacks. Existing demos use short 5–8 second loops and pause/replay.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish
review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.

## Boundaries

No backend, deployment, generated-client configuration, or business-model change.
Retain existing anchors, ID/EN, WhatsApp/email destinations and four demo scenarios.
No fabricated outcomes, customer portraits, testimonials, or replacement logos.
